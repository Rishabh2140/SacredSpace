## Project Overview

**SacredSpace** is a digital ecosystem designed to revolutionize religious and spiritual experiences, with a **special focus on pandals**. Our platform allows devotees, religious service providers, and community organizations to **create, explore, and interact with virtual pandals**, bridging traditional religious practices with modern technology.

### Mission Statement
To provide a **digital sanctuary for pandals**, where devotees can explore, participate, and connect with their faith community globally, while enabling murtikars, pujaris, and samiti members to manage and showcase their religious events and craftsmanship online.

### Vision
To become the world's leading platform for **virtual pandals**, fostering immersive experiences, global accessibility, and interfaith engagement.

## Pandal-Centric Features

### 🎪 Virtual Pandal Hubs
- **360° Immersive Tours**: Devotees can explore pandals, temples, mosques, and churches in interactive 3D environments.
- **Interactive Navigation**: Move freely within virtual pandals to view idols, ceremonial setups, and decorations.
- **Multi-Perspective Views**: Multiple camera angles for detailed viewing of events, rituals, and thematic setups.
- **Festival & Event Integration**: Each pandal can host special festivals, ceremonies, and cultural events, fully digitized.

### 🖼️ Pandal Media & Content
- **Image & Video Galleries**: High-resolution images, videos, and VR-ready media of pandals and idols.
- **Live Streaming Integration**: Stream aarti, puja, and other ceremonies directly from pandals.
- **Time-Lapse & Event Recap**: Post-event videos and photo timelines for devotees to relive the experience.
- **Decor & Thematic Documentation**: Showcase craftsmanship, decorations, and cultural themes in detail.

### 🏆 Pandal Engagement
- **Devotee Interaction**: Comments, likes, virtual offerings, and prayers within the pandal space.
- **Pandal Reviews & Ratings**: Devotees can rate pandals for experience, decoration, and spiritual ambiance.
- **Virtual Donations**: Support samiti committees or specific pandal initiatives with secure online payments.
- **Event Calendar**: Track all upcoming pandal festivals and ceremonies.

### 🛠️ Pandal Management for Samiti
- **Event Planning Tools**: Schedule rituals, track volunteer participation, and manage pujaris and performers.
- **Resource Management**: Track decorations, materials, and idols contributed by murtikars.
- **Attendance Tracking**: Record virtual participant engagement and interaction metrics.
- **Content Publishing**: Upload photos, videos, live streams, and festival highlights for devotees.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Measure engagement, donations, and community reach per pandal.

### 🌐 Pandal Discovery
- **Search & Filters**: Search by region, festival type, or pandal size.
- **Trending Pandals**: Highlighted based on views, engagement, and donations.
- **Recommended Pandals**: AI-powered suggestions for devotees based on interests, faith, or previous interactions.
